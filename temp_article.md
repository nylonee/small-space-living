## Why Go Over the Sink?

Standard wooden or plastic dish racks eat up a shocking amount of counter. A typical rack occupies roughly 40x30cm — about the same footprint as a small chopping board. In a kitchen with just 1-2 metres of total counter, losing that space every time you wash up is a real problem.

Over-sink racks solve this by using the area above your sink, which is otherwise dead space while you're not actively washing. They sit directly over the basin, letting water drip straight into the sink rather than pooling on your counter. The result: you keep your prep area clear and your dishes dry at the same time.

Drying mats offer a different solution — they roll up and store away when not in use, so they take zero permanent counter space. The three products below cover both approaches, so you can choose what fits your kitchen best.

## Buying Guide

When choosing a dish drying solution for a small UK kitchen, weigh these factors:

**Sink width:** Over-sink racks typically need at least 40cm of sink width. Measure your sink before buying — a surprising number of UK flat sinks are narrower than you think, especially if they're off the shelf from a budget kitchen fit.

**Water drainage:** The best setup channels water directly into the sink. Look for over-sink racks with a slight tilt or drain board channel. For mats, ensure the surface underneath is waterproof or use a trivet.

**Dish capacity:** If you're cooking for one, a rack holding 4-6 plates is plenty. For two people washing up once a day, go for the Vunegos (holds more vertically) or combine the Famiware rack with the mat.

**Storage when not in use:** Over-sink racks can be lifted off and stored in a cupboard or against a wall. Drying mats roll up into a tiny cylinder. Consider how much space you have for the product when it's not actively drying.

**Material:** Chrome steel is durable and cheap but can rust if the coating chips. Bamboo looks great and resists bacteria but needs occasional oiling. Microfiber mats are washable and absorbent but need replacing every 1-2 years.

## FAQ

**Q: Will an over-sink rack fit my IKEA sink?**
A: It depends on the model. Most IKEA sinks (Domsjö, Havsen, Älmhult) are standard width and will work with expandable racks like the Vunegos. Measure your sink width to be sure.

**Q: Can I use a drying mat on a laminate worktop?**
A: Yes, but make sure the mat is fully dry underneath after each use, or place it on a trivet. Prolonged moisture on a laminate seam can cause swelling.

**Q: Do over-sink racks block the tap?**
A: Most sit far enough back that the tap is still usable, but you won't be able to fill a large pot or kettle while the rack is in place. Lift the rack off temporarily — it takes two seconds.

**Q: Are bamboo racks hygienic?**
A: Yes. Bamboo has natural antimicrobial properties. Wash the rack monthly with mild soap and water, and oil it every few months with food-safe mineral oil to prevent cracking.

**Q: How many plates can a compact dish rack hold?**
A: A typical over-sink rack holds 6-8 plates and 4-6 bowls. Check the product dimensions before buying — some budget racks only hold 4 plates.

**Q: What if my sink is too narrow for an over-sink rack?**
A: Use a drying mat or a compact countertop dish rack instead. The STAUB mat works on any flat surface and fits kitchens with the smallest sinks.
