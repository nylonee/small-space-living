## Why Choose a Standing Desk Converter for a Small Space?

Full standing desks are fantastic, but they're bulky. A standing desk converter sits on top of your current desk, lifting your monitors and keyboard to standing height when you need a change. When you want to sit back down, it lowers again — all without moving furniture around. For a small flat where every metre counts, that's a game-changer.

Standing desk converters are also more affordable than full electric standing desks. You get the health benefits of alternating between sitting and standing — better posture, less back pain, and improved circulation — without the £400+ price tag or the footprint of a motorised frame. For UK renters, they're also much easier to take with you when you move compared to a bulky dedicated standing desk.

## What to Look for in a Standing Desk Converter

Before we dive into specific recommendations, here's what matters for a small UK home office:

**Size and weight capacity** — Measure your existing desktop. Most converters are 26 to 36 inches wide and weigh between 10 and 20 kg when loaded. Make sure your desk can handle the combined weight of the converter plus your gear. A solid rule of thumb: your converter should be at least 5 cm narrower than your desk.

**Height range** — Look for at least 15 inches of height adjustment so the converter works for both seated and standing positions. A good converter should bring your monitors up to eye level when you're standing — roughly 38 to 42 inches from the floor, depending on your height.

**Dual monitor support** — If you use two screens, check that the converter is wide enough and has enough weight capacity. Most models in the £100 to £160 range can handle a laptop plus one external monitor, while wider models take two monitors side by side without a squeeze.

**Ease of transition** — Gas-spring lift mechanisms are the gold standard. You want a smooth, tool-free transition that doesn't require you to clear your desk before switching positions. If the transition is fiddly, you'll stop using the converter after a week.
