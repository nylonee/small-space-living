## Buying Guide

### For Over-Sink Cutting Boards

**Measure your sink first.** Before buying, measure the width of your sink from outer edge to outer edge. Expandable boards usually cover 24-34 inches, but check the specific range of the model you want.

**Check the weight capacity.** An over-sink board should feel solid, not flimsy. Both the Decuwat and Bamboo boards here are well-rated for everyday chopping and slicing.

**Consider the juice groove.** If you prep a lot of vegetables or meat, the juice groove is essential — it stops liquid from running onto your floor or clothes.

**Think about storage.** The beauty of these boards is that they can hang on a wall or stand upright when not over the sink. They take up almost no storage space.

### For Magnetic Knife Racks

**Choose a good location.** Mount the strip on a wall away from children and pets but within easy reach of your prep area. A tiled backsplash above the counter is ideal.

**Check the adhesive.** The 3M adhesive on the Kinlo and Enpuan is strong, but make sure your wall surface is clean, dry, and smooth. Textured tiles may need a more permanent mounting option.

**Consider knife size.** Both strips hold standard chef's, bread, and utility knives. If you have very large or very small knives, check the overall length of the strip.

**Don't overcrowd.** Leave small gaps between knives for easy removal — you don't want to knock three knives off the wall trying to grab the one you need.

## FAQ

**Q: Will an over-sink cutting board damage my sink?**
A: No — the non-slip grips are designed to sit on the rim of the sink without scratching. Just make sure the board is dry before placing it.

**Q: Can I use an over-sink board with an undermount sink?**
A: Yes, but check the rim clearance. Most expandable boards work with both drop-in and undermount sinks as long as there's enough edge to grip.

**Q: How strong are the magnets on a magnetic knife rack?**
A: Both the Kinlo and Enpuan use magnets strong enough to hold standard kitchen knives securely. The Kinlo has slightly stronger magnets but both are safe for daily use.

**Q: Will a magnetic knife rack damage my knives?**
A: Actually, magnetic strips are better for knife edges than traditional knife blocks — there's no blade-on-blade contact, and the edge isn't rubbed against wood or plastic when pulling the knife out.

**Q: Are these easy to install in a rented flat?**
A: Yes — both magnetic knife racks use 3M adhesive backing. No drilling required. Just ensure the wall is clean before sticking, and they'll come off cleanly when you move out.

**Q: Can I mount a magnetic knife strip on tiles?**
A: Yes, tiles are the ideal surface. Clean the tile with rubbing alcohol first for the best adhesion. Avoid rough or heavily textured tiles.

**Q: Is bamboo better than plastic for an over-sink cutting board?**
A: Yes — bamboo is naturally antimicrobial, harder than plastic (so it resists knife marks), and looks much better in a kitchen. Both the Decuwat and Bamboo boards here are high-quality bamboo.
