Why an Air Fryer Makes Sense in a Small Kitchen

Counter space is precious in a small kitchen, and a good air fryer earns its spot in a way a second oven or a deep fat fryer never could. Because hot air circulates quickly around the food, you get the texture of frying with a fraction of the oil — and most small air fryers preheat in under three minutes, so they're faster than a conventional oven for everything from frozen chips to salmon fillets.

Running costs matter too. A typical 1,500-watt air fryer uses noticeably less electricity than a full oven, which has to heat a much larger cavity. For students watching their bills and couples in energy-conscious households, that difference shows up on the meter — especially if you use the air fryer most days. And because the baskets are non-stick and dishwasher-safe on most models, cleaning takes seconds rather than the half-hour oven scrubbing session.

Buying Guide

Before you buy an air fryer for a small UK kitchen, think about these four things:

Capacity. For one person, a 3- to 4-litre basket is plenty. For two, look at 3.8 to 5 litres — the Ninja's 3.8-litre basket and the COSORI's 4.7-litre basket both handle two portions comfortably. Only choose 5.7 litres (like the Instant Vortex) if you'll regularly cook bigger batches; the extra size costs counter space.

Footprint. Measure your worktop and the gap under your wall cupboards before ordering. Compact models like the Ninja AF100UK fit neatly under most cupboards; larger models need more clearance above for airflow, so don't push them right against the wall.

Controls. Dial controls (Ninja) are dead simple; digital presets (COSORI, Instant Vortex) take the guesswork out of common foods. Both work well — it's about whether you prefer simplicity or convenience.

Cleaning. Look for a dishwasher-safe, non-stick basket. All three models here lift the basket out for easy washing, which makes a big difference when you're using the air fryer several times a week.

FAQ

Is an air fryer cheaper to run than an oven?
Yes, generally. Air fryers heat a much smaller cavity, so they reach temperature faster and use less electricity overall — especially for small portions. For one or two people, an air fryer will typically use noticeably less energy than heating a full oven for the same meal.

Can I cook for two people in a small air fryer?
Yes. A 3.8-litre basket (like the Ninja AF100UK) handles two generous portions of chips, chicken, or fish. If you want extra headroom for batch cooking, go up to 4.7 or 5.7 litres with the COSORI or Instant Vortex.

Are air fryers healthy?
They use a fraction of the oil of deep frying — often a teaspoon or less — while still producing crispy food. They're a straightforward way to cut down on oil without giving up the texture you enjoy.

Do I need to preheat an air fryer?
Most models, including all three here, preheat in two to three minutes. Some have a dedicated preheat function; with others you can simply add a couple of minutes to your cook time.

Which air fryer is best for a student or a couple on a budget?
The Ninja Air Fryer 3.8L AF100UK at £79.99 is the best-value all-rounder for small households. If you want more capacity and presets for £10 more, the COSORI 4.7L is the step-up choice.
