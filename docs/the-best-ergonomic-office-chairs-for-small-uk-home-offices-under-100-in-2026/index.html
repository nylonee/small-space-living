Why a Good Ergonomic Chair Matters in a Small Space

When your desk is small, you might be tempted to just grab any old dining chair or cheap stool. Don't. Here's why a proper ergonomic chair is even more important in a compact home office:

  • Space efficiency: Ergonomic chairs are designed to tuck under your desk, making the most of limited floor area
  • All-day comfort: Small spaces mean you're likely using the same spot for hours — you need proper lumbar support
  • Posture protection: Without room to move around, you're more likely to stay static in a bad position
  • Multi-use: A good desk chair rolls easily between your desk and a nearby table for dining or side tasks

The key features to look for are adjustable height, lumbar support, and a breathable back. Let's look at the best options under £100.

FAQ

Are office chairs under £100 worth buying?
Absolutely — the Amazon Basics chairs prove you can get genuine ergonomic features (adjustable height, lumbar support, breathable mesh) at this price. The build quality won't match a £500 Herman Miller, but for a small home office they're excellent value.

Should I get a high-back or mid-back chair?
High-back if you're tall (5'10"+) or want full shoulder support. Mid-back if you're shorter, prefer more freedom of movement, or have a very compact space where a big chair feels overwhelming.

Will these chairs fit under a standing desk converter?
Yes — the Amazon Basics High-Back has flip-up armrests that let you slide it under any standard desk or standing converter. The Mid-Back has fixed armrests but its compact size means it fits most setups.

How do I set up a small desk ergonomically?
Three golden rules: (1) Set your chair height so your feet rest flat on the floor, (2) position your screen so the top third is at eye level — use a laptop stand or monitor arm to achieve this — and (3) keep your keyboard close enough that your elbows are at a 90-degree angle. An under-desk keyboard tray can help if your desk surface is cramped.

Do these chairs require assembly?
Both Amazon Basics chairs require basic assembly (attaching the base and backrest), but it's straightforward with included tools and takes about 15-20 minutes. The instructions are clear and all hardware is included.
